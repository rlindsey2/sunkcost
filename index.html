The small print that isn't small

    Every number on this page comes from a field in a JSON file or from the formula shown above. Nothing is fetched live. Prices are the cheapest active OpenRouter endpoint on the date checked; hardware prices are the maker's US list price, or the launch price for discontinued machines.
